Visionary Software · Product Brief

A live sound console, run by plain English.

Visionary Mixer is Visionary Software's first product built from day one for sighted and blind engineers alike. Type what you want, the way you would say it, and the app does it on a real Behringer X32-family console: the same app, the same speed, whether you read the screen or hear it through VoiceOver. In active build.

01The gap

Every church, club and venue runs on a board built for eyes.

Digital mixing consoles put hundreds of settings behind screens, encoders and pages. That is fine with sight. Without it, most of the board is out of reach.

  • Live sound is visual by design.Levels, sends, EQ curves, routing and meters live on screens, scribble strips and layered pages. A blind engineer can hear the mix perfectly and still not be able to find the control that fixes it.
  • Remote apps are remote controls.The phone and tablet apps engineers use today put the same knobs on a touch screen. They are built for sighted users, with little or no real screen reader support and no plain-language control.
  • Sighted engineers lose time too.During a show, every page flip to find a send or a filter is time the band and the room are waiting. Saying it is faster than finding it.
  • Two engineers, two different tools.Until now, a blind engineer and a sighted engineer at the same console needed different workarounds. Visionary Mixer is one app they can both reach for, at the same speed.
  • Live sound is hireable work.Church tech teams, venues and touring bands all need engineers. Closing this gap turns a blind engineer into a valuable, hireable choice for whoever books the room.
02What Visionary Mixer changes

Say what you want. The board does it.

"The snare needs more in wedge 2." "Mute the keys and bring the pastor up 3." "Why can't I hear the guitar in wedge 2?" The app reads the sentence, checks it against the console it is connected to, and makes exactly that change, then says what it did. Everything runs on the Mac, with no internet needed.

i. It knows the board

The app reads the connected console: every strip name, the routing, what each mix feeds, and what is already set. It never changes what you did not ask for, and a tuned compressor or EQ is never silently replaced; it asks first.

ii. An engineer at your side

Beyond the faders: "why can't I hear X" diagnosis, sound check help, feedback tools, setup review, system tuning for your speakers, stereo width, presets, scenes and the console's own library.

iii. Safe, offline and spoken

A safety backup before the first change, undo, and a plain refusal (with the reason) for anything the console model cannot do. Every control works with VoiceOver, and every change is spoken. No account, no internet, instant.

Verified on the practice console — the receipts

  • "mutr the keys and bring the pastor up 3": the keys muted, the pastor up 3 dB, and the app said "I read mutr as mute."
  • "now mute it" right after a change to the lead vocal muted the lead vocal.
  • "high pass the choir at 100" set the filter on both choir channels.
  • "What's the kick fader?" answered with the live value.
  • "Why can't I hear the guitar in wedge 2?" named the exact cause (the send was off) and offered to clear the way.
  • "Turn on phantom power on bus 2" was refused with the reason: buses have no phantom power.
  • Not yet in general release: the release gate is zero wrong actions on every test set, and some unusual wordings are still being closed.
  • One console family today (Behringer X32 and Midas M32). More consoles come through the board importer.
  • Shared learning between users is built but not switched on yet.
03Where it stands

In active build. Built to grow with every console.

A board importer reads a new console's controls into a catalog, and the screens and the chat are built from that catalog, so each new console gets the whole app without retraining. More consoles are being added over time.

Designed to keep getting smarter

Meaning, not fixed phrases.

The chat understands requests by meaning and is checked against hundreds of thousands of ways of saying things. It is designed to keep learning: wordings that work can be shared between users' apps, anonymously and with no strip names, so every copy gets smarter over time.

04Why this matters for Visionary

AccessBridge, FLSpeaks and OBSSpeaks open professional software that sighted users already had. Visionary Mixer is the company's first product built for both from the very first line: a blind engineer can run a full show alone, and a sighted engineer gets a faster way to drive the same board. One product, one market that includes everyone at the console.
