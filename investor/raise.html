Investor Brief · Visionary Software LLC

Four doors. One key.

Visionary Software LLC opens the professional software and gear the world already runs on to blind and low-vision people — turning what you'd read with your eyes into what you hear. One method, proven four times over: AccessBridge is in testers' hands, FLSpeaks is in active build, OBSSpeaks is feature-complete and entering testing, and Visionary Mixer, our first product built for sighted and blind engineers alike, is in active build. Every part of the case has its own page below: the company, the products, the market, the raise, and the founder.

—The products at a glance

One method. Four professional tools.

Each product has its own full brief: the gap, the fix, the numbers, and a film of it working.

Product one · Shipping

AccessBridge

Serato DJ Pro 5 and djay Pro, playable entirely by ear. Of Serato's 124 interface controls, a screen reader could read two — now it reads all of it.

Read the product brief → 100,000+ lines · 1,500+ tests · Serato DJ Pro 5 complete Product two · In build

FLSpeaks

Full spoken access to FL Studio, the world's most-searched DAW — closed to screen readers by Image-Line's own admission, opened by a live MIDI bridge.

Read the product brief → 461 commands · 597 live plugin params · verified live Product three · Entering testing

OBSSpeaks

OBS Studio, the software behind most of the world's livestreams, patched at the source to be fully usable by ear.

Read the product brief → 35 patches · 7 console tabs · 15-chapter masterclass Product four · In build

Visionary Mixer

Our first product built for sighted and blind engineers alike: run a Behringer X32-family live sound console from plain sentences, at the same speed whether you read the screen or hear it. Fully offline.

Read the product brief → 38,879 lines · 460 tests · 3-minute product film
