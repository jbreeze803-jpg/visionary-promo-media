Already on the line

I'm not asking you to bet on an idea — the proof is already built, four times.

Before asking for a cent, I built four accessibility products alone: months of full-time work, unpaid, on my own 2021 MacBook Pro M1, with test hardware bought out of pocket and zero outside funding. All four are real, working software today: AccessBridge is in testers' hands, FLSpeaks is in active build, OBSSpeaks is feature-complete and entering testing, and Visionary Mixer, our first product built for sighted and blind engineers alike, is in active build. The $450,000 is not seed money for a pitch — it is fuel for a fire that is already lit, four times over.
