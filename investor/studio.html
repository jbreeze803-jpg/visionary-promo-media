02Visionary Bus

The mastering chain, on any DAW's master bus.

VST3 · AU · Standalone

Visionary Bus ports the Mastering Suite's entire signature chain into a real-time plugin — parity-tested against the original engine to −130 dBFS. It's a controls-only insert on purpose: no custom GUI to fight a screen reader, every parameter lands in the host's own accessible FX panel. Try the same Vibe library, plus the Amount macro that blends it in.

The shipped plugin runs the full 31-stage chain — 10-band EQ, multiband, dynamic EQ, width, transient shaping, de-esser, exciter, console voicing, and a look-ahead maximizer — with 56 Vibe and 56 Character presets, each with an Amount macro that layers in at DSP time, exactly as it does here.

03Visionary Slice

13 lanes. Any rhythm, no MIDI.

VST3 · AU · Standalone

Slice fuses what three separate plugins do — drawable modulation, time/half-time effects, and glitch-stutter editing — into 13 lanes, each just a Shape, a Rate, and a Depth. No MIDI, no drawing: everything locks to host tempo, and the engine computes every lane's value live from its shape and phase — exactly what the bars below are doing. Volume, Filter, Pan, and Drive are wired to real audio here; the rest animate live exactly as the engine computes them.

Ready

The shipped plugin's full shape set includes Hold, Gate, Off-beat, Ramp Up/Down, Sine, Pump, three Euclidean densities, Random, Accelerate, and four eccentric shapes (Stab, Trill, Steps, Chaos) — this demo uses a representative subset. Rate range here is 1/16–1/2; the shipped plugin runs 1/32 to 4 bars, including triplet grids for jersey and trap feels.
